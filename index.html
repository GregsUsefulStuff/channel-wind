Which wind model should you trust?

Four NDBC buoys around the Santa Barbara Channel and Channel Islands. Each was validated against years of actual forecasts from six weather models; that proven-best combination updates automatically and drives the live speed + direction pick below. Click a buoy to see both.

The best model isn't the same everywhere: ICON is the least accurate model in the channel itself, but the most accurate at both outer-island buoys — 15–20 miles away.
